OW2 ULT DMG CALCULATOR

this calculator calculates damage for one second of an ult i.e reapers ult does 550 dmg per use but this calculator calculates the dmg his ult can do during 1 second of doing so

emre and cassity's ults dmg counter counts at the highest dmg possible

torbjurn and sigma's ult dmg is their initial cast (as this is the only part that can be boosted)

echo, hanzo, mei, symmetra, sombra, widow, sojurn, doomfist, mauga, wrecking ball, zarya, junkrat, soldier, are all excluded since their ult does not outright damage or can be boosted

the support characters juno, kiri, lifeweaver, mercy, lucio, mizuki, wuyang, brig, illari, jetpack cat, and zenyatta are excluded because they do not dmg or its dmg can not be boosted by dmg amplification.
